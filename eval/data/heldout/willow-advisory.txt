WILLOW ADVISORY ARRANGEMENT

Willow Advisory advises Oak Retail under this arrangement.

1. Engagement
Willow Advisory will meet with Oak Retail managers and will deliver written recommendations about staffing. Oak Retail will make those managers available. This engagement section does not set price, claims, or an end date.

2. Money and promises
Oak Retail shall pay Willow Advisory within twenty days of each invoice, and Oak Retail shall also cover Willow Advisory against outside claims that arise from Oak Retail's instructions, including claims by Oak Retail's customers. Both sentences in this section are operative. A late payment does not erase the covering sentence, and the covering sentence does not change the number of days.

3. End of work
Either side may end the engagement for convenience by giving thirty days of notice. Work already performed remains payable. This ending right is mutual.
