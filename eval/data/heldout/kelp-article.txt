KELP GOODS SUPPLY

Kelp Goods sells dried goods to Juniper Market.

1. Orders
Juniper Market may request dried goods in writing. Kelp Goods confirms a request before packing it. Delivery dates in a confirmation are estimates only.

Article IV — Compensation
Kelp Goods' entire compensation for a mishap stops at the amounts Juniper Market paid during the previous calendar year.

2. Credits
The most Kelp Goods can be asked to pay when an ordinary accident happens is a scheduling credit. That credit is not a yearly compensation figure.

3. Samples
Kelp Goods may send a sample before a production run. A sample is not a promise about later runs.
