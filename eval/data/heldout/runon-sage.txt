SAGE FIELD NOTES

Sage Field surveys land for Birch County.

1. Survey
Sage Field will walk the parcel, mark the corners, and deliver a map. Birch County will provide gate keys. The walk happens in daylight. The map names the corners. Staff will carry water. The county clerk receives a copy. Neighbors may watch from the road. No sample of soil is promised. The map is paper. Extra visits require a new note. Rain delays the walk. The truck stays on the lane.

2. Payment Terms
Birch County shall pay invoices within eighteen days. The price is stated on the order. Late amounts are still due. A receipt is sent after payment. The clerk may ask for a copy of the invoice. No other charge is added for the map.
