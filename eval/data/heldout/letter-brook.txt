BROOK MILL SERVICES

Brook Mill advises Cinder Bakery.

1. Work
Brook Mill will inspect the ovens and write a short report.

(a) Fees
Cinder Bakery shall pay invoices within ten days.

(b) Secrecy
Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret for three years.

(c) Cap
Aggregate liability shall not exceed the charges paid during the prior six months.
