BRAMBLE SUPPLY TERMS

Bramble Goods and Fern Market enter this supply arrangement for packaged goods.

1. Orders
Fern Market may send written requests for packaged goods. Bramble Goods confirms a request before it prepares anything. Delivery dates in a confirmation are estimates. Fern Market supplies counts, destinations, and any special handling notes. Bramble Goods may decline a request that it cannot staff. The parties will discuss timing when a confirmation and a request disagree. Nothing in this orders section sets a payout, a credit, or a duty to hide records.

Article IV — Payout
Bramble Goods' entire compensation for a mishap stops at the amounts Fern Market paid during the previous calendar year.

2. Credits
The most Bramble Goods can be asked to pay when an ordinary accident happens is a scheduling credit. That credit is not a yearly compensation figure.

3. Samples
Bramble Goods may send a sample before a production run. Fern Market returns comments on the sample. A sample is not a promise about later runs.
