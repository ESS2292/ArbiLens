ASH POTTERY WORKSHOP

Ash Pottery teaches a class to River School.

ARTICLE V — FEES
River School shall pay invoices within eight days.

WARRANTY
Except for the express promise to teach the class, the workshop is provided as is.

(a) Notice
Either party may terminate for convenience by giving five days' notice.

Ownership Of Lesson Plans
Lesson plans created for the class belong to River School after the class is paid for.
