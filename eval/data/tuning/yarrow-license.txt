YARROW LICENSE

Yarrow Studio prepares drawings for Maple Press.

1. Grant
Yarrow Studio lets Maple Press use the drawings while a statement of work is open. Use is limited to Maple Press's own packaging. This grant does not say who keeps the drawings after they are paid for. The grant also does not move Yarrow Studio's older templates.

Ownership of new drawings
Once Maple Press has paid, the drawings created for the job belong to Maple Press.

2. Tools
After the bill is settled, the studio keeps the pictures made for this assignment inside its template drawer. Yarrow Studio also keeps its older templates, and those templates stay with the studio rather than moving to the client.
